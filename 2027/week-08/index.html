RE:
Week 8 Proceedings

WHEREAS the Week 8 matchup period, running Monday, November 16 through Sunday, November 22, has concluded, the Office of the Commissioner hereby enters the following into the record.

Item 1. The Commissioner has determined that fantasy hockey is stupid, and is considering the dissolution of this League. Owners are asked to state, in writing, whether anyone actually cares about silly numbers on a spreadsheet?

Item 2. The Commissioner's franchise, Commish Campi, is in a state of crisis, having fallen 74.5 to 94.0 to the Chiang Mai Knockouts for a fourth consecutive loss and a 3-5 record. The Commissioner is cranky this morning and requests forgiveness for Item 1. He presumes this sentiment is shared by the other 3-5 franchises: Liberate The Big Squid, The atlanta thrashers, the Danimals and Maximum Penalty.

Item 3. HutsonBrothersRazzleDazzleShow, having been passed in last week's power rankings despite continuing to win, took this personally and deposited 130.9 points upon the Danimals, who tendered 40.7. Both figures are the extremes of the period. Ryan's record now stands at 6-2 on a four-game win streak. Animal Control has opened an investigation into animal cruelty charges after you put the Danimals in a body bag.

Item 4. Entering Thanksgiving week, the Commissioner records his actual thanks for this League, its text thread, and a spread-out group brought together by hockey and the mutual tolerance of one another's college allegiances. This past weekend marked the fourth anniversary of the passing of Jordan Jackson, longtime friend to many here and an original Hockey Squad participant and enthusiast. He would love that we are keeping this going.

Item 5. The Turkey Bowl convenes at 8:00 a.m. Thursday at Lewis Creek Park. All are welcome.

Lineups are to be set in good faith, dissolution notwithstanding. So ordered.

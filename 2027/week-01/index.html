WHEREAS the Week 1 matchup period, running Tuesday, September 29 through Sunday, October 4, 2026, has concluded, being the first (1st) of twenty-three (23) such periods, the Office of the Commissioner hereby enters the following into the record.

Item 1. The League is hereby declared back in session. The Commissioner notes that it was a pleasure to see everyone at the draft. The draft was conducted across the members' varied time zones, and the League made it work as best it could, which the record will reflect as good enough.

Item 2. The power rankings will be issued in this new format, on a trial basis. Feedback is welcomed and will be received by the Office of the Commissioner.

Item 3. As is custom every year, the season's opening power rankings shall first honor the Champions of the League. All remaining franchises shall then be ranked on Week 1 performance. Owners are advised that the Tier system is subject to change from week to week, without notice and without appeal.

With twenty-two (22) weeks remaining, all franchise owners are reminded that lineups are to be set in good faith. So ordered.

Memorandum

TO:
All Franchise Owners
FROM:
The Commissioner
DATE:
Monday, November 2, 2026
RE:
Week 5 Proceedings

WHEREAS the Week 5 matchup period, running Monday, October 26 through Sunday, November 1, 2026, has concluded, the Office of the Commissioner hereby enters the following into the record.

Item 1. The League takes judicial notice of Halloween and of the excellent kiddo costumes entered into the group chat. Owners holding further such exhibits are directed to submit them. Special commendation is issued to Adam Degginger for leading the charge with an all-time family KISS costume.

Item 2. The atlanta thrashers (Caden), 74.5 to 66.1 over the Danimals, and 🎶Hey! Hey baby (Luc Dubois)🎶 (Tuvin), 96.5 to 72.2 over Commish Campi, are hereby recognized for their first wins of the season. The League no longer contains any big-time winless losers.

Item 3. The Commissioner finds that numerous franchises prioritized counting candy rather than goals and assists, yielding a bunch of mid-tier performances. Only two (2) teams broke 100: HutsonBrothersRazzleDazzleShow, 106.8 to 93.0 over Maximum Penalty, and the Tapps Tiger Muskies, who did the Monster Mash all week and defeated Liberate The Big Squid 139.3 to 96.2, the high score of the year. Something must be done to stop this swamp monster. Any willing party is ordered to drain Lake Tapps.

Item 4. The Apples division is dividing pretty cleanly, with the Tapps Tiger Muskies at 5-0 and the Chiang Mai Knockouts at 4-1. The Dudes division could not be tighter: Commish Campi, JAIGNER 69ers and HutsonBrothersRazzleDazzleShow stand tied for first at 3-2, with Tuvin, at 1-4, only two (2) games back.

Item 5. The League extends best of luck to all dads attempting to sneak candy from their kids' stashes this week.

All franchise owners are directed to govern themselves accordingly. So ordered.

D. CampiCommissioner, Hockey Squad

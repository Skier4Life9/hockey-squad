RE:
Week 6 Proceedings

WHEREAS the Week 6 matchup period, running Monday, November 2 through Sunday, November 8, has concluded, and the results thereof have been duly certified, the Office of the Commissioner hereby enters the following into the record.

Item 1. The League did collectively deposit 1,044.6 points, approximately sixteen percent (16%) above average and the first single-week total exceeding 1,000 points this season. Said total would have ranked as the second-highest regular week of last season, and constitutes a bounce back from the prior week's diminished scoring.

Item 2. The Tapps Tiger Muskies, having attended the HutsonBrothersRazzleDazzleShow, did sustain their first loss of the season, 119.7 to 116.4. The Muskies are hereby awarded both the Tough Luck award and the Closest Game award. Kenton is congratulated on his Participation Trophies, now 5-1.

Item 3. Maximum Penalty did deposit 127.3 points upon the Chiang Mai Knockouts, who tendered 102.8, securing both the High Score and the Biggest Blowout. Dan Degg is commended, as is Player of the Week Cutter Gauthier, whom the League finds to be a cold-blooded killer.

Item 4. All parties are reminded that Veterans Day falls on Wednesday. Unlike most leagues, this League sells no merchandise for profit thereon, but encourages each owner to thank a Veteran for their service, without whom Fantasy Hockey would not be possible.

With seventeen (17) weeks remaining, all franchise owners are reminded to set lineups in good faith. So ordered.

D. CampiCommissioner, Hockey Squad

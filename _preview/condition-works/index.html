TO:
All Franchise Owners
FROM:
The Commissioner
DATE:
Monday, March 2, 2026
RE:
Week 19 Proceedings

WHEREAS the Week 19 matchup period, running Monday, February 23 through Sunday, March 1, has concluded, and the results thereof have been duly certified by ESPN, the Office of the Commissioner hereby enters the following into the record.

Item 1. Maximum Penalty did knowingly and with malice aforethought deposit 98.9 points upon the Danimals, constituting the highest score of the period. The Danimals, as the party of the second part, tendered 47.8 points, which the League accepts without further comment.

Item 2. The Central District Cityboys, having lost each of their first eighteen (18) contests, are hereby recognized as the winners of a regular-season game, 75.6 to 69.5, over the Tapps Tiger Muskies. The Muskies are advised that this matter is now a permanent part of their file.

Item 3. The Commissioner defeated Liberate The Big Squid 97.4 to 87.6. The Commissioner wishes to state, for the avoidance of doubt, that this outcome had no bearing whatsoever on the rankings below.

With two (2) weeks remaining and six (6) playoff berths outstanding, all franchise owners are reminded that lineups are to be set in good faith. So ordered.

D. CampiCommissioner, Hockey Squad
